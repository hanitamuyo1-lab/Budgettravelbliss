What we do

Budget Travel Bliss is a free travel planning tool that helps you discover affordable destinations around the world, estimate the real cost of your trip, and convert currencies on the go — all in one place. No sign-up required, no hidden fees, no fluff.

Every destination in our database comes with a realistic daily cost estimate based on accommodation, food, local transport, and activities — so you can plan with confidence before you book a single flight.

How costs are calculated

Daily cost estimates reflect a mid-range budget — a comfortable private room, two meals at local restaurants, and one activity or attraction per day. Backpacker and luxury multipliers adjust from there. All figures are in USD and updated periodically based on traveller reports and public pricing data.

Currency rates shown in our converter are indicative. Always confirm the live rate with your bank before travelling.
