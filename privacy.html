Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Budget Travel Bliss ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our website and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

Budget Travel Bliss is a static, client-side website. We do not operate servers that store personal data. The following information may be collected automatically by third-party services we use:

  • Usage data — pages visited, time spent, browser type, and device information, collected via standard web analytics.
  • Cookies — small files placed on your device to remember preferences such as your last calculator inputs. You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

2. Information You Provide

If you subscribe to our newsletter, we collect your email address solely to send you travel updates. We do not sell, rent, or share your email with third parties.

3. Third-Party Services

Our site loads content from the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Unsplash — destination photography (unsplash.com)
  • Wikipedia / Wikimedia — city images loaded via the public Wikipedia API (wikimedia.org)
  • Google Fonts — typography (fonts.google.com)
  • Lorem Picsum — placeholder images (picsum.photos)

These services may collect your IP address and browser information when your browser requests content from them. We do not control their data practices.

4. How We Use Information

  • To operate and improve the website
  • To understand which destinations and features are most useful
  • To send newsletter updates (only if you subscribed)

5. Data Retention

We do not store personal data on our own servers. Any data collected by third-party analytics tools is subject to their respective retention policies. Newsletter subscriptions can be cancelled at any time.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal data held about you. To exercise these rights, please reach out through our website.

7. Children's Privacy

Budget Travel Bliss is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date at the top. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
